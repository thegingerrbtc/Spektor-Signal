08 Before you join

Questions traders usually ask

The short version: Spektor publishes market-structure alerts with price guidance. It does not decide your position size, leverage, stop or execution.

What is included in a signal?+

Direction, symbol, signal tier, timeframe, timestamp and price guidance, presented in the same concise format shown above.

How frequently are signals published?+

There is no fixed schedule. Signals arrive only when the relevant market and timeframe satisfy the system's breakout conditions.

Which markets are monitored?+

BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP and DOGE across 1H Fast, 4H Flagship and 6H Sentinel—18 symbol-by-tier feeds in total.

Does Spektor provide stops or leverage instructions?+

No. Spektor identifies breakout structure and price context. Members remain responsible for risk, sizing, leverage and execution.

Is this an automated trading service?+

No. Membership provides alerts and research access. The paper-trading system is a simulation and does not place real trades.

What is simulated versus forward-tested?+

The historical report is a costed simulation. The forward dashboard appears only after at least five positions close after deployment, with no historical positions imported.
