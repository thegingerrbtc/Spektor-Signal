04 Method & provenance

Two records. One lineage.

Every execution row carries its originating signal ID. The relationship is preserved; the measurements are never blended.

01

Signal generated

Frozen tier rules produce an entry and lifecycle result at native cadence.

02

Signal archived

Raw gross capture is stored exactly, without any execution assumptions.

03

Bot consumes

Independent spot and futures accounts size the trade from current equity.

04

Fill simulated

Twenty basis points adverse fill per side and published Aster taker fees are applied.
